# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its code of
conduct. The full text is published at:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique the work and not the
person, and do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and any community channel — and to public interactions where someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer via the contact on their GitHub
profile: <https://github.com/Vishalsng112>

Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may warn, remove content, or ban
a participant, and will explain the decision to the reporter. Retaliation
against a reporter is itself a violation.
